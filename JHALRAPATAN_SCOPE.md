# Team Gopal Singh Harnawada Gaja — Jhalrapatan Assembly App

## Product scope
This package is scoped to **Assembly Constituency 198 — Jhalrapatan, Rajasthan** while retaining reusable Jhalawar district modules from the legacy app.

## Data integrity rule
No generated LGD code, population, officer, budget, project, beneficiary or contact data is to be presented as official. A record is `Verified` only when it has an authoritative source and verification date.

## Official source hierarchy
1. Chief Electoral Officer, Rajasthan / ECI — constituency, electoral-roll, polling-station and election information.
2. Government of India Local Government Directory (LGD) — local-body/village identifiers and hierarchy.
3. Rajasthan Jan Soochna — schemes and public-service information.
4. Rajasthan Shala Darpan — government-school directory and education information.
5. District Jhalawar / Rajasthan Government departments — district administration, orders, contacts and departmental information.
6. Other official Rajasthan department portals — only for the subject they administer.

## Important
Government records change. `Verified` means verified against the cited source as of the recorded verification date; it is not a guarantee that the government source will never change.
