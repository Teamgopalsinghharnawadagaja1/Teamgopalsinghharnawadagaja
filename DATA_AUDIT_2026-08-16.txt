TEAM GOPAL SINGH — DATA & PRODUCTION AUDIT
Date: 2026-08-16

CRITICAL FINDING
The previous build contained a function that generated missing Gram Panchayat records using counters and synthetic population, LGD codes, villages, facilities, schemes, budgets and representatives, while marking those records as verified. Those values cannot be treated as factual civic information.

CORRECTION APPLIED
1. Removed synthetic generation of LGD codes, population, households, villages, facilities, schemes, budgets and representatives.
2. Unknown records are now explicitly marked needs_verification.
3. Added dataStatus/sourceUrl fields to GramPanchayat.
4. Existing local records are no longer automatically presented as verified.
5. Added a data-integrity policy to README.
6. Added authoritative provenance references to Government of India LGD and District Jhalawar Government of Rajasthan.

IMPORTANT
This makes the application safer and factually honest, but it does NOT magically make every data field complete or current. 100% factual completeness requires importing and periodically re-verifying authoritative datasets (LGD, Rajasthan Panchayati Raj, district/department portals, and official scheme/project records), plus production SMS, storage, deployment and release configuration.

DO NOT publish the app as “100% verified” until those imports and checks are completed.
